🌈 Why Does Online Safety Matter?

We use the internet every day to learn, play games, watch videos, talk with friends and discover new things.

Being safe online helps us enjoy these activities while protecting our privacy and personal information.

  • Keep personal information private
  • Use strong passwords
  • Think before clicking links
  • Be kind to others online
  • Ask an adult when something feels wrong
🌐

You Are a Digital Superhero!

🛡️

Super Safety Tips

Remember these simple rules whenever you're online!

🔑 Use Strong Passwords

Create long, unique passwords. Avoid using your name or birthday.

🔒 Protect Your Privacy

Never publicly share your home address, phone number, school details or location.

🔗 Think Before You Click

Don't click strange links or download unexpected files.

👤 Be Careful With Strangers

Be careful when talking to people you don't know in real life.

🚫 Block & Report

If someone is bullying or bothering you, block and report them.

🧑‍🏫 Tell a Trusted Adult

If something makes you uncomfortable, talk to a parent, teacher or trusted adult.

⚠️

Watch Out for These!

Knowing about online risks helps you recognize them before they become problems.

💬

Cyberbullying

Hurtful, threatening or embarrassing behavior using digital platforms.

🎣

Phishing

Fake messages or websites that try to trick you into giving information.

🎭

Fake Profiles

Some accounts may pretend to be someone they aren't.

📱

Privacy Risks

Sharing too much personal information can put your privacy at risk.
